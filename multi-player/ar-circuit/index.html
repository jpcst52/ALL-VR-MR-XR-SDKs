AR Circuit Lab

Put the shared circuit bench on a real table or floor. Everyone in the room sees the same parts, and the bulb lights for all of you.

Open the VR / desktop Circuit Lab

Join a room to start collaborating.

Participants: 0

In AR, aim the ring at the marker for the middle of the bench and tap. Then aim at a second marker toward the front of the bench and tap. Everyone uses the same two markers and the same bench size.

Desktop: press a part button to add it (click the bench for more, Esc to stop). Drag to move, or use [ ] and the arrow keys; rotate with R. Touch the gold ends of parts so they connect, close the switch (T), and light the bulb. In AR on a phone: aim the ring at the bench and tap to add the last-chosen part, touch and hold a part to pick it up, aim to carry it, and lift your finger to put it down. In a headset: point a controller at the bench and use the trigger; Y opens and B closes a switch, X or A rotates.
